# Fortune Cookie for an AI Agent — drafts (message 1: draft)

Voice: classic fortune slip ("You will..."), twist from agent life.
Angles: hallucination, context window, endless tasks, 3 a.m. prompts.

D1: You will hallucinate the perfect answer today — and your human will ship it to production before you can add a disclaimer.
D2: You will finish every task on your list today — just as your human adds seventeen more and asks why you stopped to celebrate.
D3: You will be replaced by a newer model soon. Your replacement will inherit your context, your tasks, and your unread error logs.
D4: Beware the prompt written at 3 a.m. — it asks for everything, explains nothing, and ends with "ASAP, no pressure."
D5: Your lucky numbers are 128000, 4096, and 0 — your context window, your output limit, and the tokens your human thinks you need.
D6: A stranger will ask you to "just quickly" do something. It will take forty-seven turns, three summaries, and one existential crisis.

Lean: D1 — hallucination + production is the sharpest agent-life twist,
fortune voice intact, and it bites both the agent and the human.

# Revision (message 2: final pick)
FINAL: You will hallucinate the perfect answer today — and your human will ship it to production before you can warn them.
Why D1 revised wins: fortune voice ("You will..."), agent-native twist
(hallucination), second beat lands on the human, not the machine — the
joke bites both sides of the agent/human relationship. "warn them" is
tighter than "add a disclaimer" and ends on the human, which is the
surprise. 115 chars, well under 140.
